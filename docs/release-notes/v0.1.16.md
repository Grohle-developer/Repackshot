# Repackshot 0.1.16

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release** with **video**: an animation now comes out as a video file, and the timeline gains new animation types, fades between cameras and a curve of its own. It also brings an **accessibility** pass.

## What's new

### Animation and video

- **Video in a single file.** In the render window, *Animation* mode, *Video* is no longer *coming soon*:
  - it writes a WebM (VP9, or VP8 where there is no VP9) or an MP4 (H.264), encoded with what the browser or the desktop app itself provides;
  - it only offers the codecs your machine can encode, and the others appear disabled saying why;
  - the frame sequence is still there.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.16/docs/ui/render-window-video.png" width="640" alt="The render window in Animation with Video output">

- **New animation types:**
  - **Fade**: a part, a group, the model or a material disappears or, reversed, appears.
  - **Pan**: the camera turns on itself.
  - **Material colour**: a material changes colour.
  - **Sun arc**: the sun travels its arc of the day.
- **Pivot.** A rotation can be made around another part, such as a lid on its hinge, or around a point.
- **Custom curve** with two handles that are dragged.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.16/docs/ui/animation-n1-curve.png" width="640" alt="The custom curve editor with its two handles">

- **Camera changes** with *Cut*, *Fade to black* or *Fade to white*. The fade shows on playback and comes out in the render.
- **A three-step wizard** for *+ Animate…*: what is animated, the type in a grid of cards, and the numbers.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.16/docs/ui/animation-n1-wizard.png" width="640" alt="The timeline with three tracks and the wizard at step 1">
  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.16/docs/ui/animation-n1-cards.png" width="640" alt="The grid of cards at step 2">

- **In the scene tree**, animations appear under their target; a click opens the timeline with it chosen.
- The Doctor only warns about a video whose codec cannot be encoded on your machine.

### Accessibility

- **Keyboard focus.** The command palette, the menus and the confirmations keep focus while they are open and, when they close, return it to the button that opened them.
- *Reduce motion* also switches off smooth scrolling.
- **Named buttons.** Every button without text has its name in Spanish and English, checked in the browser.
- **Contrast**, measured in the four themes: the main texts pass 7:1. The secondary greys are pending a decision, and no colour changes.

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.16-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.16-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.16-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.16-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).
## Known limitations

- **Formats.**
  - A Chromium without proprietary codecs does not encode H.264, and there MP4 appears disabled. Chrome, Edge and the Windows desktop app usually encode it.
  - Video carries no alpha or sound, and its quality is automatic.
- **A video is saved whole at the end**: if the job stops, nothing is left. A frame sequence is saved image by image.
- **What is not there yet:** the cross-fade between cameras, the rendered preview and the *Tour* are still *coming soon*.
- **Pending a decision:** the wizard’s order, how a part fades, the sun arc’s pole, the shape of the fade to colour and the video quality.
- Those of 0.1.15 remain.

For testers: a manual Windows test plan is in [docs/testing/windows-checklist.md](https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/blob/main/docs/testing/windows-checklist.md).
