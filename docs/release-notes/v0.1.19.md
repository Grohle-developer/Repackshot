# Repackshot 0.1.19

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release.** Its new features:

- an **element editor** for the traced lens;
- the focal-length switch **Keep the size of what is in focus**;
- the **Wood** and **Fabric** nodes in the material editor;
- a **batch that travels with its studio** inside the project.

## What's new

### Lens editor

- **Edit elements…**, in *Camera ▸ Lens ▸ Traced lens*, opens the lens in a window of its own. You work on a copy: the catalogue lens never changes.
  - The lens cross-section, with each element numbered; a click picks one.
  - For the chosen one: **add** one in front or behind, **turn it 180°**, **delete it**, make it smaller or larger, or adjust its aperture alone.
  - A table with the two radii, the thickness, the glass, the diameter and the air to the next one.
  - Each edit is an undo entry.
- **The engine checks every change** and gives the design focal length, the flange distance, the f-number, the image circle, the distortion and the light towards the corner.
  - A lens that forms no image, lets no light through or has a negative gap says why, and where. It is not applied, but you can keep editing.
- **Apply to the camera** sets it as the camera’s own lens; **Save to library…** saves it in *Mine*. It goes back and forth with the project.

### Focal length

- **Keep the size of what is in focus**, under *Focal length* in the *Camera* section and in the palette.
  - **Off** (as in a real camera): when the focal length changes, the camera stays where it is and the framing changes.
  - **On**: the camera moves closer or farther along its axis so that what is in focus keeps its size in the image. The perspective changes, not the size. It follows the camera’s focus: the distance, the point or the object.
  - A line below says which is active. With a locked, orthographic or panoramic camera there is nothing to move, and it says so.
  - It is saved with each camera. Earlier projects open it off.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.19/docs/ui/c2-focal-switch.png" width="339" alt="The Camera section with the Keep the size of what is in focus switch on, below the focal length">

### Wood and fabric with nodes

- **The *Wood* and *Fabric* nodes** in the material editor, with the same parameters as their families: rings, figure, pores, fibres and knots; weave, threads per cm, relief and twist.
- **Switching a wood or a fabric to *Nodes*** gives its node already connected, and going back to *Simple* leaves it as it was.
- **In *Full* and in renders**, the graph paints the same as the family. In *Fast* the rings are now visible.
- **The wood example** is now *Oak (Wood node)*, and looks like the *Wood* family.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.19/docs/ui/material-nodes-wood.png" width="640" alt="The material editor with the Oak (Wood node) example: the Wood node connected to the output">

### Batches

- **A job’s studio travels with the project.** A batch over saved studios renders the same on another machine, or in another copy of the portable build, even if its library does not have those studios. Before, it failed with *The studio “…” is not saved any more*.
- The images of those studios (HDRI, background, textures) go in the project **only once each**.
- In the queue, a studio that came with the project has **Add to my library**.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.19/docs/ui/batch-queue-adopt.png" width="640" alt="The queue of a project opened with an empty library: the two jobs rendered in their studios and the Add to my library line">

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.19-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.19-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.19-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.19-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).

## Known limitations

- **Lens editor.**
  - The mirror lens and the anamorphic adapter cannot be edited.
  - Without the engine, the lens can be edited but not applied.
  - The window can be moved, but it does not resize or remember where it was.
  - When passing over the lens list, the small window shows the catalogue lens that the edited one came from.
- **Focal length.**
  - With tilt, the size is kept where the plane of focus crosses the axis, not across the whole plane.
  - With a very wide-open traced lens, what is in focus can vary by a few millimetres.
  - The animation’s *Zoom* clips do not move the camera.
- **Wood and fabric.**
  - The *Fuzz* of the *Fabric* node does not yet reach the material.
  - The family’s wood in *Fast* is still its average colour with the knots; the node one already shows its rings.
- **Batches.** A job from a project older than this version does not carry its studio until you edit it or add it again.
- Those of 0.1.18 remain.
