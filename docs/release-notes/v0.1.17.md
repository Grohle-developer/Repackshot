# Repackshot 0.1.17

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release** with four new features:

- the **interior camera**: straight verticals, 360° panorama, fisheye, plan and elevation, and automatic exposure;
- **batches and passes**: cameras per studio, the alpha pass, passes as files and a ZIP;
- the light catalogue reorganised into **Sets and Lights**;
- **arrows on every value**.

## What's new

### Interior camera

- **Straight verticals.** In the *Camera* section. With the camera tilted, the walls do not fall away: the camera is rendered level and the lens is shifted to keep the framing. The *Interior* type switches them on.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.17/docs/ui/k3-verticals.png" width="640" alt="A room with the camera looking down and the vertical edges parallel">

- **Projection:** *Perspective*, *Orthographic*, *Panorama 360° (2:1)* and *Fisheye*, and the *Elevation* and *Plan* views. In Fast, the panorama is drawn from the six faces of a cube, with the same map as the engine.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.17/docs/ui/k3-panorama.png" width="640" alt="The 360° panorama of a room in a 2:1 image">

- **Visible to camera.** In the *Part* section. The camera stops seeing the part, but its shadows and bounced light remain. This is how the plan is made: the hidden ceiling still lights the room.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.17/docs/ui/k3-plan.png" width="640" alt="The plan of the room seen from above, without a ceiling and with the same light">

- **Automatic exposure.** In the *Image* section:
  - *Brightness (EV)* becomes the compensation;
  - there are two presets, *Bright day · EV 7* and *Evening lamps · EV 5*;
  - the *Exterior* slider darkens what is seen through the window without removing the light that comes in.
- **Eye height** with two presets, *Standing* (1.5 m) and *Seated* (1.1 m), and **interior lenses** of 24, 28 and 35 mm.

### Batches and passes

- **Passes**, where it said *coming soon*: *Alpha*, *Depth*, *Normals* and *Part ID*.
  - Each one is a file next to the image, in 32-bit EXR or 16-bit PNG.
  - The part ID carries its legend in a `.json`.
  - Alpha is new in the engine.
- **Batches per studio.** Choose the scene as it is, a saved studio or **all**. With *All cameras*, 2 cameras × 2 studios give four images. The window says the size of the batch and warns if two files would have the same name.
- **ZIP.** A switch gathers everything from a *Render* into a single ZIP, which downloads when the last job finishes.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.17/docs/ui/batch-window.png" width="640" alt="The render window with a batch of two cameras by two studios, three passes and ZIP">

- **Fix.** *Start* with an empty queue no longer leaves the queue blocked for the next ones.

### Lights: Sets and Lights

- **Two groups.** The *Light* section separates the catalogue:
  - **Sets**, what builds background, floor, cards or pool of light, with the *Product type* and *Subject* filters;
  - **Lights**, what only places lights, with the *Style* filter.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.17/docs/ui/light-sets.png" width="480" alt="The Sets group with the Product type and Subject filters">

- **If there are already lights**, choosing another setup asks what to do with them:
  - **Replace**;
  - **Hide**: they stay switched off and are switched on again in the *Lights* list;
  - **Add**.

  Each option undoes in one go. *Remember my choice* is changed in *Settings*.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.17/docs/ui/light-question.png" width="480" alt="The Replace, Hide or Add question when choosing a setup with lights in the scene">

- **Thumbnails.** All have the same size. A button to the right of the section header makes them small, medium or large.

### Values

- **▲ ▼ arrows on every numeric field.**
  - A click steps up or down: with `Shift`, a tenth; with `Ctrl`, ten.
  - Held down, they repeat.
  - Each press undoes in one go.
- **Sliders where there is a fixed range**, with their value and their arrows beside. For example:
  - the angles and the aperture of an own light;
  - friction and bounce;
  - the JPEG quality and the *denoise* strength;
  - the orbit and zoom speeds in *Settings*.
- The render window’s **Time** field also has its arrows, one minute per step.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.17/docs/ui/u15-light-fields.png" width="640" alt="The inspector of an own light with fields with arrows and sliders">

### Fixes

- *Settings ▸ Preferences*: the *Downloads* and *Choose folder…* buttons are no longer cut off.
- The summary line at the foot of the render window now wraps to two lines instead of being cut off.
- *About*: the credits stay with the public literature the engine follows and the third-party licences.

The full list, with its limitations, is in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`. All the screenshots, by version and by feature, are in [`docs/GALERIA.md`](https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/blob/v0.1.17/docs/GALERIA.md).

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.17-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.17-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.17-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.17-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).

## Known limitations

- **Interiors.**
  - There is no WASD walkthrough.
  - The panorama is rendered whole, not in strips.
  - In Fast nothing is drawn on top of a panorama.
  - *Interior* does not lower the camera to eye height: the presets are in the *Camera* section.
  - Automatic exposure is measured by the application: a scene exported and rendered elsewhere carries only the compensation.
- **Passes and batches.**
  - Alpha is a mask of the objects: it is not the alpha of a render with a transparent background when there is glass or a floor that catches the shadow.
  - Animations do not write passes.
  - The ZIP is assembled in memory: closing the page before it finishes loses it, and it does not exceed 4 GiB.
- **Pending a decision:**
  - the eye heights and the automatic measurement;
  - what counts in the alpha;
  - the order of the batches;
  - which setups go in *Sets* or in *Lights*;
  - the look of the arrows in narrow fields.
- Those of 0.1.16 remain.
