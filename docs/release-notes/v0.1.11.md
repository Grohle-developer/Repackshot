# Repackshot 0.1.11

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release** that improves the **ambient particles** (*Scene ▸ Ambient*): dust shines against the light, rain no longer speckles the product and particles cost less.

## What's new

- **Dust against the light.** Dust motes, pollen and dust cloud scatter part of the light forward, like real dust: with a light behind the product, the motes light up in its beam. With side light they look almost as before.
- **Rain without dots.** Drops and bubbles no longer cast caustics on the product and the floor: light crosses them straight, with their transparency. The camera still sees them refract, also through glass and in mirrors.
- **Cheaper particles.** Each camera ray tests only the particles of its piece of the image, also with blur: dust goes from costing 22 % more than the scene without it to 8 %, and dust cloud from 21 % to 10 % (measured in engine instructions, which do not depend on the machine’s load). A scene without particles does not change a pixel.
- Projects saved earlier open with the new dust of their preset.

The full list, with its limitations, is in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.11-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.11-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.11-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.11-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).

## Known limitations

- **Rain** still costs about 1.6 times the scene without it (before, 2): the expensive part is no longer finding the drops but the light that passes through them. Inside the streaks, with a side light, some grain remains that cleans up with more samples.
- How much light the dust’s backlit shine takes away is a proposal pending review, and *Fast* does not show it.
- Those of 0.1.10 remain.

For testers: a manual Windows test plan is in [docs/testing/windows-checklist.md](https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/blob/main/docs/testing/windows-checklist.md).
