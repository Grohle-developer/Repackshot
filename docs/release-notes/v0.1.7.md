# Repackshot 0.1.7

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release.** It brings **real wood, fabric and edge bevel** in *Full* mode and in renders, **rough materials that no longer lose light**, **scattering media** (milk, wax, soap, juices, cream, translucent plastics), the **chromatic dispersion of glass**, the **traced lens** with 16 real lenses, **effect filters on the lens**, **setup thumbnails redone** with this version’s engine and **fixes from a bug report** (focus with a click, the colour of a fabric in nodes, the `.repack` extension, the AI’s attempts). It is the first version reached with *Update from the app*.

## What's new

- **Real wood.** The 13 woods in the catalogue draw their rings, grain, pores and relief at physical size (rings of 2 to 5 mm depending on the species), with the roughness stretched along the grain. **Knots** with their dark end grain, bark and crack: pine has four, cherry and walnut one. New controls: *Scale*, *Ring contrast*, *Pores*, *Knots* and their size, variation and darkness.
- **Fabric thread by thread.** The 12 fabrics weave their warp and weft threads in plain weave, twill 2/2, twill 3/1 (denim), satin, canvas, knit and felt, with *Threads per cm* of their own for each fabric (cotton 40, denim 24, satin 100…).
- **Edge bevel.** The *Edge bevel* field no longer says *coming soon*: it rounds the light on sharp edges, in reflections and in shadows. Saved projects gain it without touching anything (the Doctor already set 0.25 % of the part’s size on them).
- **Rough materials no longer lose light.** Metal, plastic gloss, varnish and rough glass return the light that bounces between their microfacets: a white metal of roughness 1 goes from reflecting 36 % to 100 %, and images gain about 14 % of light.
- **Scattering media.** *Internal scattering*, *Scattering colour* and *Direction* in a glass’s inspector fill the solid with a medium that scatters light inside. Nine new materials: white HDPE, natural PP, frosted acrylic, wax, glycerine soap, milk, orange juice, apple juice and cream.
- **Chromatic dispersion.** *Abbe number* on glass: a solid glass splits light into colours when refracting, and its caustics are coloured too. New material: **Perfume crystal**.
- **Traced lens.** *Camera ▸ Lens*: *Simple lens* or **traced lens**, one of the 16 real lenses in the catalogue (triplet, Petzval, double Gauss, telephoto, wide angle, fisheye, macro, catadioptric, variable-aberration soft lens, anamorphic adapter…), chosen by character (*Clinical, Soft, Swirl, Vintage, Anamorphic, Macro, Wide, Tele*). The engine traces every ray through its surfaces, so the **distortion**, the **vignetting**, the **bokeh shape** at the edge (cat’s eye, bubble, swirl) and the T-stop are those of that lens. The section shows its numbers, two curves and the **cross-section with its elements**; *Advanced* keeps the non-physical controls and *Save lens as…* adds it to your library. In *Fast* the distortion and vignetting are approximated, and a click lands on what is under the cursor even with a fisheye.
- **Lens filters.** *Camera ▸ Filters*: up to three per camera, among white, black and gold diffusion, glow, softening, net, 4-, 6- or 8-point star and streaks, each with its strength. The halo carries the bokeh and the focus, and a light outside the frame veils the image as in a real camera.
- **Fixes from a bug report.** *Focus with a click* no longer undoes itself with the same click (nor does a typed focus when clicking in the scene). A fabric switched to *Nodes* keeps its colour. Projects are saved as **`.repack`** (same content; `.repack.json` files still open). The queue says *attempt 2 of 4* and the reason when the AI guard repeats, and the AI preview no longer starts over while a model downloads.
- **Setup thumbnails redone.** The 88 thumbnails come from this version’s engine: the stray dots in *Long strip and black edge*, *Black on black* and *Warm-cool cinema* are gone. The pool of light of *Gradient backdrop with a pool* goes up to remain twice as bright as the background.

The full list, with its limitations, is in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.7-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.7-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.7-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.7-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release, which *Settings ▸ Plugins ▸ Install* downloads from there; it can also be installed from the file.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).

## Known limitations

- **Textures, labels and node materials** still show only in *Fast*: *Full* and the render use the average of each output until the engine reads them (M2b and M3b). The mapping controls appear when an image is put in the slot.
- *Fast* does not paint the wood’s rings or the fabric’s threads, only their average colour (and the knots, darkened), nor does it round edges: they show in *Full* and in the render.
- A scattering medium converges slowly with a strongly marked direction, and around a dispersive crystal there is colour grain at few samples.
- **From the bug report of 2026-10-14**, what remains is either waiting for its phases (texture and node mapping in *Full*, the live view in the queue, the elements window when passing over a lens) or a decision (the focal length, the studios, the drop, the lens element editor): it is triaged in `docs/TRIAGE.md`. The AI attempts loop and the denoise that washes out colours could not be reproduced here (see point 79 of the Windows test checklist).
- **Traced lens**: at an equal number of samples its bokeh has more grain than the thin lens’s (each sample carries a colour); tilt is not traced (shift is). Which lens gives which bokeh and their prescriptions are pending review.
- The limitations of earlier versions remain: the new desktop features **have not been tested on a real Windows machine**, and the first real AI image on a graphics card is still pending.

For testers: a manual Windows test plan is in [docs/testing/windows-checklist.md](https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/blob/main/docs/testing/windows-checklist.md).
