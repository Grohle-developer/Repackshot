# Repackshot 0.1.5

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release.** It brings **own caustics and denoising**, **own lights that are placed and moved in the viewport** (and cameras), the **own prompt and AI variants** with a system prompt in *Settings*, and a **guided tour** the first time the app opens. The first real AI image on a graphics card, pending since 0.1.4, is still to be produced.

## What's new

- **Caustics.** *Render window ▸ Quality ▸ Caustics*: *Automatic* (the default), *On* or *Off*. Light that passes through glass or bounces off polished metal reaches the shadow, with our own photon map and no stray dots. *Automatic* turns them on when there is glass or polished metal and a small light. *Caustic quality* is under *Advanced*.
- **Own denoising.** *Advanced ▸ Denoise*: *Off*, *Viewport*, *Render* or *Both* (the default). It respects the edges of each part and each material. With *Also save without denoise* both images are saved. In the *Full* viewport, the *Denoise* button turns it on or off.
- **Own lights.** *Lights ▸ Add light*: area (rectangle, disc or sphere), point, spot or sun. Each with its colour, power, shadow and visibility, and *Aim at* the product’s centre, a point or a part. *Save setup as…* saves them with the setup.
- **Lights and cameras in the viewport.** While you work with lights or cameras, the viewport draws their outlines (dashed except the chosen one). They are chosen with a click and moved with the gizmo, and the camera also with its target. **Look through** (`Ctrl ⇧ L`): orbiting, panning and zooming move the light or the camera, and `Esc` goes back.
- **AI: own prompt and variants.** In the AI section of the render window, *Own prompt* (up to 400 characters) is added to the scene’s one; the protections stay the same. *Variants* (1 by default, up to 8): one engine render and several AI images with consecutive seeds. The results viewer shows them in a strip, with *Save this variant* and *Save all*.
- **AI: system prompt.** *Settings ▸ AI post-processing ▸ System prompt*: a few words for every image, which favour realism and respect lights, shapes and depth of field, with *Restore default*.
- **Guided tour.** The first time the app opens, a zone-by-zone tour of all the options, with *Skip tour*. It comes back from *Help ▸ Guided tour*.
- **Runtime notice.** With an AI model downloaded and no runtime, the render window and *Settings* say so, with a button to install it.
- **The installer also asks for the runtime** when an AI model is chosen, and the first start downloads it. The portable build offers it on its first start, ticked by default.

The full list is in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.5-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.5-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.5-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.5-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).

## Known limitations

- **The first real AI image on a graphics card is still untested**, now also with the own prompt and the variants. With SD 1.5 the own prompt almost never fits (it reads 77 tokens).
- **The installer page with the runtime has not been seen on a real Windows machine**: the installer cannot be run here.
- Anyone updating from another version sees the guided tour once.
- IES profiles are still *coming soon*. Points, spheres and spots give no caustics.
- Caustics cost about 24 % more time, do not come from the environment, and with a transparent background the shadow catcher does not pick them up.
- The limitations of earlier versions remain, above all that the new desktop features **have not been tested on a real Windows machine**.

For testers: a manual Windows test plan is in [docs/testing/windows-checklist.md](https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/blob/main/docs/testing/windows-checklist.md).
