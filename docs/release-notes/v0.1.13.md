# Repackshot 0.1.13

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release** with **adaptive sampling**: a render can finish when its noise falls below a threshold, and the engine spends samples where they are most needed.

## What's new

- **Finish by noise.** *Render ▸ Quality ▸ Finish by* gains **Noise**: you choose a threshold (0.5 to 5 %, 1 % by default) and the render ends when every part of the image falls below it, with the samples as a cap. The window estimates how long it will take.
- **Adaptive sampling.** After 16 samples on all pixels, the engine distributes the following ones in 8 × 8 blocks according to the noise each has left: clean areas (the background, matte parts) stop receiving samples and glass, highlights and soft shadows keep going. At equal time, the error drops by 16–17 % in the test scenes (a backlit glass bottle and a single key light).
- The viewport meter says *Adaptive · min–max samples*, and the queue’s live view, the noise that remains against the target.
- Denoising takes into account how many samples each pixel has.
- The same render gives the same pixels with any number of threads, on the desktop and in the browser. Renders by samples or by time do not change.

The full list, with its limitations, is in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.13-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.13-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.13-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.13-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).

## Known limitations

- The adaptive gain is smaller than planned (16–17 % less error at equal time, against the 40 % that was aimed for); how to distribute the samples better is still to be decided.
- The time estimate comes from the last pass of the *Full* viewport with the same threshold; with *Denoise* on, the first line of the meter may show the samples of the filtered image.
- Those of 0.1.12 remain.
