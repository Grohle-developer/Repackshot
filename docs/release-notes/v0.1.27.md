# Repackshot 0.1.27

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release.** Its new features:

- the **update window** now shows a small **What changed**, offers what Settings offers, and can **skip a version**.

## What's new

### The update window, completed

- The window that asks about a newer version at startup now shows a small *What changed*: the first lines of the new version's own notes, in plain words.
- Its options are now those of *Settings ▸ Updates* for the version: *Install and restart* (or *Download*), *Release notes* — and an install started from the window is followed in the window: its progress, *Cancel the update*, *Restart now* when the restart waits, and a failure with its reason and *Try again*.
- *Skip this version*: the version is remembered and is not announced again — neither in the window nor in the status bar — while a newer one still is. Settings keeps offering a skipped version: skipping silences the announcement, not the update.

The full list, with its limitations, is in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`. The screenshots, by version and by feature, are in [`docs/GALERIA.md`](https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/blob/v0.1.27/docs/GALERIA.md).

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.27-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.27-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.27-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.27-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).
