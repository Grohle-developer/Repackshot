# Repackshot 0.1.6

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release.** It brings **image textures and labels**, a **node-based material editor**, **studio sets** with photographic intent, the **improved thin lens** with a bokeh chooser, **TIFF and RAW (DNG)** output, the **image aspect ratio in the camera**, **plugins** with the first one, the developer’s **film simulation**, the engine’s **per-point light choice**, **configurable exploded views** with a new timeline, **rigid bodies** with *Drop*, **ambient particles**, **live denoising** with its strength and **edge smoothing**, and **updating from the app**.

## What's new

- **Textures and labels.** glTF, OBJ+MTL, FBX, DAE, 3MF, USD and 3DM bring their materials with their maps; a material’s inspector has the *Textures* section (colour, roughness, metal, normal, alpha, emission; five projections in mm) and *Add label…* places an image with alpha on the part with one drag. They show in *Fast*; the engine will read them in the next version.
- **Node-based material editor.** *Nodes…* in the inspector opens the new window: a canvas with 19 node types, a preview sphere per node, examples (marble, wood, brushed metal, leather, plastic with a label…) and *Simple ↔ Nodes* without losing anything. The *Fast* view draws them; the engine will evaluate them in the next version.
- **Studio sets.** The *Sets* style: 16 light setups with background, floor, cards, pool of light, exposure and a suggested camera; the 15 product types are a set; five new materials and a *Gradient* background.
- **Camera ▸ Bokeh and advanced optics.** 20 bokeh types with a thumbnail rendered by the engine; blades, pupil profiles, obstruction, cat’s eye, anamorphic, tilt and shift, chromatic fringes.
- **TIFF and RAW.** The render window offers TIFF (8, 16 or 32-bit float, with a transparent background) and RAW as linear DNG 1.4.
- **The aspect ratio belongs to the camera.** Each camera carries its own aspect ratio (3:2, 4:3, 16:9, 1:1, 9:16, 21:9… or custom); the viewport frames it and the render window follows it.
- **Plugins.** *Settings ▸ Plugins* installs plugins from the registry or from an `.rpk` file (encrypted, with an activation key). The first one is **aintfilm · film simulation** (closed licence, a preview of a photo-editing app in development by the same developer): in the render window, after the AI, a carousel of recipes with *Type*, *ISO* and *Group* filters, a slider, split preview, *Apply* and *Apply to render jobs* (in an animation, each frame with its own film seed).
- **Per-point light choice.** The engine chooses the light for each point by what can illuminate it: the sparks in sets with a small light disappear, 1.4 to 3.8 times faster at equal noise.
- **Exploded views and timeline.** Linear (separate or stack), radial, spherical and level-based exploded views, with exceptions, order, speed, smoothness, distance and reverse; a three-zone timeline (tree of what is animated, bars with snapping, properties), repeats and tracks in the viewport.
- **Drop and the physics bar.** Right click ▸ *Drop*: the part falls with real physics and the simulation stops by itself when everything is at rest. A *Start · Pause/Resume · Stop · Reset* bar (stopped by default), a *Physics* section in the inspector; every simulation is recorded as a clip of the animation.
- **Ambient.** Dust motes, pollen, rain, snow, dust cloud, ash, bubbles and confetti, simulated in 3D and configurable (amount, size, energy, wind, gravity, seed); *Fast* shows the same particles the engine sees.
- **Live denoising and strength.** *Denoise* next to *Fast · Full* cleans the viewport while it accumulates; *Advanced ▸ Denoise ▸ Strength* blends the filtered image with the noisy one (0 % does not filter).
- **Edge smoothing.** *Settings ▸ Render engine ▸ Edge smoothing*: MSAA for *Fast* and, for *Full* and the render, *None · Standard · Soft · Fine (default) · Fine + edges ×2 · ×4*.
- **Update from the app.** *Settings ▸ Updates ▸ Install and restart* downloads the new version, checks its SHA-256 and restarts with it (portable and installed); the question about unsaved changes gains *Save as…*.

The full list, with its limitations, is in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.6-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.6-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.6-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.6-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.
- **film-0.1.0.rpk**: the film simulation plugin (1 956 072 bytes, `sha256 a60dd9edea828f4ed29acd3d29b9ab1ffde384284da8c6a6a6619cc1d6ba3d8a`). *Settings ▸ Plugins ▸ Install* downloads it from here and asks for the activation key, which its developer provides. Closed licence; Repackshot’s does not cover it.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).

## Known limitations

- **Textures, labels and node materials** show in *Fast*; *Full* and the render use the average of each output until the next version (M2b, M3b). Bevel, knotted wood, thread-by-thread fabric, scattering media and lens filters are done and arrive in the next one.
- **Particles**: dust against the light barely shines in the beam and rain under a hard key light speckles the product; they cannot be selected in the viewport.
- **Physics**: rigid bodies only; *Cloth* and *Liquid* say *coming soon*. While a simulation runs the viewport switches to *Fast*.
- **Film plugin**: the developer’s activation key is required; it is a preview of an app in development.
- **Live denoising**: the launcher’s lane is one thread; MSAA 8× has not been seen drawn here.
- **Update from the app** debuts with this version: the first real update will be from this one to the next, and on Windows it has not been tested on a real machine.
- The 88 setup thumbnails were rendered before the per-point light choice; they are redone in the next version.
- The limitations of earlier versions remain: the new desktop features **have not been tested on a real Windows machine**, and the first real AI image on a graphics card is still pending.

For testers: a manual Windows test plan is in [docs/testing/windows-checklist.md](https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/blob/main/docs/testing/windows-checklist.md).
