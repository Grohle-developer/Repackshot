# Repackshot 0.1.15

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release** with two new features: the **Interior scene type**, the first step towards interiors, and the **Engine Doctor**, which looks at how the engine is running and points out what can be improved.

## What's new

### Scene type and interiors

- **Scene type.** In the inspector, *Product* section: *Product · Interior · Catalogue*. Changing it sets the starting values of each type without removing any control:
  - **Interior:** sun and sky, 16 bounces, no caustics, AgX, 512 samples that finish by noise, with denoise, and 28 mm.
  - **Catalogue:** the studio dome with a white background and soft shadow, 8 bounces, Standard, 256 samples and 50 mm.
- **It undoes in one go.** Going back to the previous type leaves the project as it was, except for what you touched in between. A loaded HDRI and a locked camera are not touched.
- **Seven interior light setups,** in the new **Interior** style: *Morning window*, *Midday window* and *Afternoon window*, *Overcast*, *Night lamps*, *Blue hour* and *White studio room*. The previous 88 setups do not change.
- **Interior Doctor.** With the *Interior* type there are seven more checks, each with its *Fix*: scale and units, opening for the sun and sky, thick window glass, a lens that is too wide, eye height, near clipping against the wall and wall normals.
- **Sets without decoration.** Choosing a recipe, or any set without decoration, no longer switches on the floor or the contact shadow. Nor does it leave the previous set’s background in the frame.

### Engine Doctor

- **New checks.** The Doctor also looks at the engine:
  - whether `rt.wasm` loaded and how many threads it has;
  - whether it runs with SIMD;
  - the denoise;
  - clipped highlights with *Standard*;
  - the *fireflies* of the *Full* viewport;
  - the video format;
  - whether the render time is enough for the requested samples, with the speed the engine measured.
- **Fixes.** What the project can change has its *Fix* and goes into *Fix all*, which undoes in one go. What cannot be fixed from the project, such as a single thread or the lack of SIMD, is explained, and if a *Settings* row limits it, a button opens it.
- **Up to date.** The Doctor runs again on undo and redo, and when what it reads from the engine changes.

The full list, with its limitations, is in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.15-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.15-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.15-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.15-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).
## Known limitations

- **Proposals pending a decision:**
  - the figures of the seven interior recipes and which is the starting one for *Interior*;
  - the thresholds of the interior checks and of *Tone*, *Fireflies* and *Budget*.
- **What comes with the next interior phases:**
  - *Interior* sets 28 mm without moving the camera; the interior camera comes later.
  - There is no room or cyclorama yet.
  - The interior checks deduce the room from the parts that are there.
- **Limits of the *fireflies* check:**
  - it is not read in *Fast*;
  - with the desktop viewport *denoise* on, they come out as “nothing to count”.
- Those of 0.1.14 remain.
