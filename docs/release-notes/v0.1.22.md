# Repackshot 0.1.22

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release.** Its new features:

- rain drops on the parts **grow, run, gather the drops in their path and hang before they fall**, with the shape of real water;
- drips and splashes **fall through the air**;
- every **AI post-processing** control now changes the result step by step, and the style is a real colour grade;
- the AI section shows a **stored example made with the Full engine**, without calling a model;
- the AI model can run **through an API** as well as on this computer.

## What's new

### Organic drops

- **Drops grow and let go.** A drop sitting on a part grows as rain lands on it and as it merges with its neighbours. It stays put until it is heavy enough for the tilt and the material of its face, then runs down.
- **A running drop gathers the drops in its path**, so it grows and speeds up as it goes. Two drops that touch flow into one: a neck shows for a moment and the water settles where it belongs, without a jump.
- **Rivulets.** On a steep face, a large running drop leaves a trail of small beads behind it, and the next drop down the same path picks them up.
- **Real shapes.** A running drop stretches along its path, with a rounder head and a thinner tail. A drop held on a slope bulges downhill. A hanging drop stretches and pinches. The shapes keep the drop's volume, and they show in *Fast*, in *Full* and in renders.
- **Hanging and dripping.** A drop that reaches an open edge stops and hangs there. Under an edge, or under a face that looks down, it grows, necks and lets go of one drop with about 60 % of its water; what stays grows again.

### Drops through the air

- **Drips and splashes fly.** The drop that drips off a part, and those a splash throws, fall with gravity and the drag of the air, and settle where they land. While they fall they show in *Fast*, in *Full* and in renders.
- **The meter says what is on the parts**: *Rain*, *Dust*, *Snow* or whatever there is together, on how many parts and, with rain, how many drops are on them and in the air.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.22/docs/ui/physics-rain-air.png" width="640" alt="The example bottle in the rain with wind, paused with drops on its body and drops in the air">

### AI post-processing that does what its controls say

- **Every control changes the result step by step.** *Light*, *Bounces*, *Wear and micro-detail* and *Style strength* now scale what the model did in their part of the image: nothing at 0, as the model made it at 50, twice as much at 100. Before, at their defaults they could only let about half of it through.
- **The style is a real colour grade**, applied by the guard itself, so it is the same with any model. Before, the six styles gave the same pixels.
- **Intervention goes further**: the default now asks the model for an img2img strength of 0.3, and up to 0.6 at 100.
- **The guard says what it did, in plain words**: where it held a change back, how much of each material's colour change it kept, and which checks made it try again more gently. Under each control, a line says what it acts on.
- **Fixed:** a model that changed nothing brightened the image slightly, and the focus check rejected legitimate wear on surfaces in focus next to an out-of-focus floor.

### A stored example, made with the Full engine

- **The preview no longer calls a model just to show the controls.** It is a stored example: a reference scene rendered by the Full engine and run through the real guard with a test model, at every step of every control. It changes as soon as a slider moves, and it says what it is and at which values it was made.
- **Preview my scene (Full engine)** runs the AI on your own scene, drawn by the Full engine.

### The AI model through an API

- **Run the model on this computer or through an API**, in *Settings ▸ AI post-processing ▸ Model by API*. The render window says which one is active.
- **Any HTTP image-to-image service**: describe its URL, headers, the body with placeholders for the image, the prompt, the strength and the seed, and where the image comes back. Two presets are included, for Stability AI SD 3.5 image-to-image and OpenAI image edits; both are written from the services' published API documentation and are marked *not verified* until they are tried against the live services.
- **The key stays on this computer.** On the desktop the app calls the API itself and the page never reads the key back; in the browser it is kept in local storage, with a warning. It is shown masked and sent only to the host it was saved for, and never written to a project, the library, a log or a message.
- **Asked before anything is sent.** The first time the AI is turned on for a host, the render window asks in place: *Allow and send* or *Not now*. The answer is remembered per host and can be revoked.
- **The same guard on every result.** A result the guard rejects leaves the engine's image unchanged.
- **Failures are worded and bounded**: a refused key, a rate limit, a time-out, a bad answer or a redirect each have their own message, and only temporary failures are tried again, at most twice by default.

### Notices

- **If the render engine is older** and cannot draw drops in the air or shaped drops, the interface says so under *On the parts*. This can happen, for example, with an old desktop launcher.

The full list, with its limitations, is in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`. The screenshots, by version and by feature, are in [`docs/GALERIA.md`](https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/blob/v0.1.22/docs/GALERIA.md).

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.22-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.22-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.22-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.22-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).

## Known limitations

- **Drops.**
  - Wind does not yet push the drops in the air.
  - A drop that reaches the side edge of a tilted face stops there instead of running along it.
  - Dynamic parts, those that move with the physics, do not get wet, and the studio floor does not get wet.
- **AI post-processing.**
  - The stored example covers one control at a time away from its defaults; when several of your values differ, the section says which.
  - What reaches the model for *Light*, *Bounces* and *Wear* is still only words; their effect is applied by the guard.
  - The measurements were made with a test model; the real models' response has not been measured yet.
- **AI through an API.**
  - Neither preset has been tried against the live service yet.
  - Only PNG answers are read, and an answer given as a link is not followed.
  - In the browser, a service that does not allow cross-origin requests fails as a network error, and the guard runs on the page's own thread.
  - Plain `http` is only allowed to this computer; a service elsewhere on the network needs `https`.
- Those of 0.1.21 remain.

For testers: a manual Windows test plan is in [docs/testing/windows-checklist.md](https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/blob/main/docs/testing/windows-checklist.md).
