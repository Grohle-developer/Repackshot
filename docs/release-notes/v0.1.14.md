# Repackshot 0.1.14

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release** with two new features: the **section view**, which cuts the part with a plane to show its interior, and **IES lights**, which illuminate with the light distribution of a manufacturer’s file.

## What's new

### Section view

- **Cutting the part.** In the *Scene* inspector, the switch in the **Section** header cuts the product through its centre, facing the camera. Inside, the plane is placed in three ways:
  - a click on the part plus an axis (*X*, *Y*, *Z* or the surface normal);
  - two points;
  - numbers.
- **What the cut looks like.**
  - What is left on one side of the plane disappears. It also casts no shadows and does not appear in reflections or refractions.
  - The cut is capped with the material’s colour, on closed solids.
  - A cut glass is seen through its cap.
- **Controls.** *Flip side*, *Cap the cut* and *Remove the plane*, all undoable. In the palette there are two actions: switch on or off, and flip side.
- **The studio is not cut.** The background, floor and cards of a set stay whole.
- **In the timeline,** the **Section** move on the Environment shifts the plane along the part: the cut can be animated.
- *Fast*, *Full* and the render cut in the same place. A scene without a plane renders as before, pixel for pixel.

### IES lights

- ***+ Add light ▸ IES…*** is no longer *coming soon*. Open an `.ies` file, or drop it on the window, and it creates a light that distributes light as the file says.
- **The inspector** shows the profile’s name, a polar plot of its shape, the peak and the beam angle. It has **Change…** and a brightness **multiplier**.
- **The profile travels inside the project,** the studios, the saved setups and the library: it opens on another machine without the file.
- **Full and the render** use the whole file. *Fast* approximates it with a spot, and the inspector says so.
- Repackshot ships no manufacturer profiles: everyone brings their own.

The full list, with its limitations, is in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.14-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.14-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.14-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.14-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).
## Known limitations

- **Section caps.**
  - *Fast* draws a glass’s cap as transparent glass; *Full* and the render refract through it.
  - A part with a material per face, a thin-walled glass and an open mesh, which shows its interior, are not capped.
  - Where the control goes, how a glass is capped and whether the studio can be cut are still to be decided.
- **AI post-processing:** its *Fast* passes (depth, normals, identifiers) do not yet know the section plane.
- **IES files.**
  - Only type C photometry files are read. A type A or B one is rejected with its message: export it as type C.
  - An IES light produces no caustics, like the point and the spot.
- Those of 0.1.13 remain.
