# Repackshot 0.1.18

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release.** Its new features:

- **click-to-focus lands on the exact pixel**;
- there are two new focus modes, **Point** and **Object**;
- the **simple lens** and the **traced** one, each with its own options;
- **easier tilt**;
- an **AI that shows more**;
- a start-up that **no longer renders without a model**.

## What's new

### Focus

- **The click focuses the exact pixel.** With *Focus with a click*, the point where you click comes out sharp in the final render:
  - with the simple lens and with the traced one, even wide open;
  - with tilt;
  - in *Full*;
  - looking through a saved camera;
  - in the interior projections.

  The engine resolves the focus with its own rays.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.18/docs/ui/f1-focus-sheet.png" width="640" alt="Three clicks before and after: the clicked point comes out sharp in the render">

- **Three focus modes** per camera, in the *Camera* section:
  - **Distance**: the usual one.
  - **Point**: the clicked point stays stuck to its part. It stays sharp even if you move the camera or the part, in the animation and in batches.
  - **Object**: the focus follows a part, a group or the model. It is also chosen with *Keep in focus* in the part menu.
- **Focus distance with a slider**, with its value and its arrows beside. If you move it in *Point* or *Object*, the camera switches to *Distance* from that value.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.18/docs/ui/f1-focus-inspector.png" width="340" alt="The Camera section with Focus Distance, Point and Object, and the distance slider">
  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.18/docs/ui/f1-focus-marker.png" width="480" alt="The marker of the focused point in the viewport">

### Simple lens and traced lens

- *Thin lens* is now called **Simple lens**.
- **Bokeh and *Advanced optics* belong to the simple lens.**
  - With a traced lens they do not appear, and choosing a bokeh no longer takes you out of the traced lens.
  - When you go back to the simple one, they are as you left them.
- The traced lens has **More options**, with its own **tilt and shift**. The engine tilts the real lens.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.18/docs/ui/o4-traced-more.png" width="340" alt="More options of the traced lens, with its tilt">

- **Easier tilt**, on both lenses:
  - the amount on a slider;
  - the direction on a dial with shortcuts: *like the floor*, *like the ceiling* or *like a wall*;
  - **Through two points…**: two clicks on the product and both come out sharp;
  - the **plane of focus drawn in the viewport**.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.18/docs/ui/o4-tilt-overlay.png" width="640" alt="The tilted plane of focus drawn in the viewport">
  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.18/docs/ui/o4-traced-tilt.png" width="640" alt="Lights over a receding floor: untilted they go out of focus, tilted they come out sharp">

### AI, lights and other changes

- **The AI shows more.**
  - The default intervention goes from 30 to 50, and the guard’s tolerances are doubled.
  - If you had the factory values, they update by themselves. If you had changed them, they are respected.
  - **The tolerances in *Settings* now really apply**, in the browser and on the desktop. Before, they were not used.
- **The viewport always starts in *Fast*.** It no longer starts rendering as soon as it opens, without a model.
- ***Interior* starts with the *Midday window* recipe.**
- **Lights.** Choosing a setup no longer asks *Replace / Hide / Add* if the lights are those of the product type, untouched.
- **Doctor.** The fix for clipping with *Standard* proposes AgX.
- **Contrast.** Secondary text reads better in the light theme.
- **Normal maps.** They can no longer point inward. The flakes of the metallic paint, which did, now come out right.

The full list, with its limitations, is in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`. The screenshots, by version and by feature, are in [`docs/GALERIA.md`](https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/blob/v0.1.18/docs/GALERIA.md).

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.18-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.18-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.18-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.18-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).

## Known limitations

- **Traced lens with distortion.** With distortion +1 at f/1.4, in the bottom-right corner the sharp zone lies about 38 px below the click.
- **Focus time.** Focusing a traced lens takes half a second per click, and can repeat up to three times while the lens “breathes”.
- **The anamorphic adapter** does not tilt, and says so.
- **Setting removed:** *Viewport mode at start-up* no longer exists; *Full* is chosen in the top bar.
- Those of 0.1.17 remain.

For testers: a manual Windows test plan is in [docs/testing/windows-checklist.md](https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/blob/main/docs/testing/windows-checklist.md).
