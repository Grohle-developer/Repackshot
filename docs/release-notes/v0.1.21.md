# Repackshot 0.1.21

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release.** Its new features:

- **rain, dust and snow** settle on the parts and on the front glass;
- drops **slide, merge and drip**;
- the *Shape* in *Ambient* is always in view;
- the front glass is visible in *Fast*.

## What's new

### Weather on the parts

- **On the parts**, in the controls of *Rain*, *Water drops*, *Dust motes*, *Pollen*, *Dust cloud*, *Ash* and *Snow*. It starts with *Start* on the physics bar, and the simulation happens on the parts.
  - Drops settle and stay stuck until they are heavy enough to slide.
  - When sliding they pass from one face to another, merge with other drops and drip off the edges. When they fall, they splash.
  - What is wet looks darker and glossier. Wood, fabric and paper absorb the water.
  - Dust and snow settle where the surface is not steeply inclined. A sliding drop cleans the dust in its path.
- **It is recorded as a physics clip** on the timeline and saved with the project. It shows in *Full*, in renders and in *Fast*.

### The front glass, wet from the scene

- **Drops and dust ▸ From the scene**, in *Camera ▸ Lens ▸ Filters ▸ Front glass*: the rain and dust from *Ambient* fall on the glass while the simulation runs. Drops merge and slide down leaving a trail, and that trail cleans the dust.
- **The glass in *Fast***: the viewport draws an approximation with the same drops and motes the engine places, and says so.

### *Ambient*

- **The *Shape* is always in view**, right after the *Size*, and the *Mix* weights go below.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.21/docs/ui/ambient-shape.png" width="640" alt="The controls of an effect with the Shape among the visible ones">

### Notices

- **If the render engine is older** and does not know how to draw the scene’s glass or the weather on the parts, the interface warns that the result will come out clean or dry. This can happen, for example, with an old desktop launcher.

The full list, with its limitations, is in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`. The screenshots, by version and by feature, are in [`docs/GALERIA.md`](https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/blob/v0.1.21/docs/GALERIA.md).

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.21-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.21-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.21-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.21-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).

## Known limitations

- **Drops on the parts.**
  - A dripping drop reaches the bottom at the same instant; it is not yet seen falling, nor are the splashes.
  - Dynamic parts, those that move with the physics, do not get wet.
  - The studio floor does not get wet.
  - On the large parts of the set, the edge of the snow and dust goes in steps of about 10 mm.
- **Dust.**
  - With the presets’ densities it is barely visible at 120 s: raise the density.
  - It is a layer; loose motes do not come out.
- **Front glass.** With a wide-open telephoto lens, the drops only veil the image. With a wide angle or the aperture closed they read as drops. A lens that looks downward stays dry if there is no wind.
- **Duration.** Each simulation lasts at most 120 s.
- Those of 0.1.20 remain.
