# Repackshot 0.1.20

Repackshot is a free, source-available 3D product renderer, in the style of KeyShot. It runs as a desktop app and in the browser, and it renders with its own engine, written in Rust with no dependencies.

**This is a preview release.** Its new features:

- the catalogue **wood** shows its rings and knots in *Fast*;
- **fabric** is woven in *Fast* when its threads are visible;
- the **drop** and the **mix of shapes** in *Ambient*;
- a **front glass** with drops and dust in front of the lens.

## What's new

### Wood and fabric in *Fast*

- **The catalogue wood now looks like wood in *Fast*:** rings, pores and knots, not just its average colour. It comes out with the same pixels as the same wood made with nodes.
- **The knots fall where the render puts them.** In *Fast* they are placed part by part, as the engine does in *Full*.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.20/docs/ui/m3d-wood-fast.png" width="640" alt="Oak and knotty pine: Fast before, Fast now and Full">

- **Fabric is woven in *Fast* when its threads are visible.** This happens with canvas, mesh, carbon or knit on a part of normal size, and the fabric keeps its fuzz. Finer fabrics stay as before, so that no moiré appears.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.20/docs/ui/m3e-fabric-fast.png" width="640" alt="Knit, canvas and cotton: Fast before, Fast now and Full">

- **Fix:** a material fade on a knotty wood now shows in *Fast*.

### Drops in *Ambient*

- **Drop shape**, in *Ambient ▸ Advanced ▸ Shape*: a round body with a tip that follows it, like a tear. It has its *Drop length* and *Tip* controls. In *Full*, the water refracts.
- **Mix of shapes:** *Shape ▸ Mix* distributes the particles among points, flakes, streaks and drops, with a weight for each shape. *Shape variation* makes some drops and streaks longer than others.
- **New preset:** *Water drops*.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.20/docs/ui/ambient-drops.png" width="640" alt="The bottle with the Water drops effect in Fast">
  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.20/docs/ui/ambient-mix.png" width="640" alt="The Mix shape: points, flakes, streaks and drops">

### Front glass

- **Front glass**, in *Camera ▸ Lens ▸ Filters*: a glass in front of the lens with water drops and dust, like a lens in the rain or a wet window.
  - Controls: *Drops*, *Drop size*, *Dust*, *Seed* and *Distance*.
  - The engine traces each drop as the water lens it is: a soft disc with the scene upside down inside.
  - Without drops or dust, the image does not change at all.
- **It is real optics.** With a telephoto lens and the aperture open, the drops only veil the image. They read as drops with a wide angle, with the aperture closed or with the glass farther away.

  <img src="https://github.com/Grohle-developer/Repackshot/raw/v0.1.20/docs/ui/lens-glass.png" width="640" alt="The bottle through a wet glass, at f/16">

## Downloads

- **Repackshot-0.1.20-windows-x64-portable.zip**: Windows portable. Unzip it and run `repackshot.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder next to the program.
- **Repackshot-0.1.20-windows-x64-setup.exe**: Windows installer, for your user only and without administrator. Data goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Repackshot`.
- **Repackshot-0.1.20-linux-x64-portable.tar.gz**: Linux portable; it needs Chromium, Chrome, Edge or Brave (and glibc 2.38 or later for the AI).
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: the SHA-256 sums of the three packages. On Linux: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`; in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\Repackshot-0.1.20-windows-x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256`.

The film simulation plugin does not change: it is still `film-0.1.0.rpk`, the asset of the 0.1.6 release.

The `.exe` is not signed: SmartScreen may stop the first start (**More info**, then **Run anyway**).
## Known limitations

- **Wood and fabric in *Fast*.**
  - A knot looks like a dark disc; its end grain and the grain around it show in *Full*.
  - The fuzz of a fabric made with the *Fabric* node does not yet reach the material.
- **Drops.**
  - They do not yet settle or slide on the parts, nor does the dust settle: that is the next phase.
  - The *Shape* is still inside *Advanced*.
- **Front glass.**
  - *Fast* does not draw it.
  - Passes and click-to-focus do not see it.
  - The scene’s drops do not wet it; it is adjusted by hand.
- Those of 0.1.19 remain.
